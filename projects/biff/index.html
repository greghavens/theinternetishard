Biff

For builders who need classification, routing, scoring, or condition checks as structured model outputs.

A PROJECT BY GREG HAVENS

THE PROJECT, IN PRACTICE

What you can do with it.

01

Choice

Ask a question with named options and receive a probability distribution over those options.

02

Score

Define an ordered scale and get a distribution over its levels, together with an expected score.

03

Condition

Ask whether a stated condition holds and receive its probability.

04

One state, many questions

The design reads a shared state and answers multiple typed questions against that context without generating a text answer.

FROM IDEA TO USE

How it works.

  1. 01

    Provide context

    Send the state you want to evaluate, such as a support ticket or document.

  2. 02

    Define the judgment

    Choose the question type and supply criteria or allowed options.

  3. 03

    Use the probabilities

    Threshold, route, or rank using the structured output in your application.
