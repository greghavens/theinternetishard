THE PROJECT, IN PRACTICE

What you can do with it.

01

Meet the agent where you work

Connect through supported messaging channels, WebChat, or the command line.

02

Enforce policy in code

Classification gates and deterministic hooks govern what the agent can read, do, and send.

03

Choose your model provider

Configure supported hosted providers or local models, with provider choices independent of the messaging interface.

04

Discover skills and plugins, or build capabilities for your own workflows.
